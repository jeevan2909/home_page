Yamaha R15 – The Sporty Icon of the Streets

The Yamaha R15 is one of the most iconic entry-level sport bikes in the Indian market. Its sharp, aerodynamic design not only makes it visually stunning but also enhances performance and control.

Equipped with a 155cc liquid-cooled engine and Variable Valve Actuation (VVA) technology, the R15 delivers smooth power across the rev range. It's ideal for both city rides and weekend thrills.

Riders love its aggressive riding posture, modern LED headlamps, and race-inspired digital instrument console. The dual-channel ABS and slipper clutch add to safety and performance on twisty roads or racetracks.

If you're looking for a bike that combines daily usability with sporty DNA, the Yamaha R15 remains an unbeatable choice in its class.

Key Features

  • 155cc Liquid-cooled Engine
  • VVA Technology
  • LED Projector Headlamp
  • Dual-channel ABS
  • Digital Instrument Console
  • Slipper Clutch
